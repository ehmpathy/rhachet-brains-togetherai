## notes

1. **minimax models**: vision claim in blueprint referenced these. at research phase, MiniMax models (m2.5, m2.7) were identified via third-party benchmarks but not confirmed on together.ai serverless model list. they may exist on dedicated endpoints or be region-restricted. catalog excludes unverified models for reliability.

2. **error handle improvements**: added to satisfy peer review guard. these improvements align with mechanic role standards but were not explicitly specified in blueprint. blueprint focused on model catalog; execution naturally expanded to include quality improvements around the changes.

3. **test coverage**: integration tests use representative sample (8 models) rather than exhaustive test of all 14. this balances coverage vs api cost. all tiers (cheap, balanced, frontier) are represented.
