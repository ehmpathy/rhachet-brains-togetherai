# self-review: has-questioned-requirements

## requirements questioned

### 1. "add ALL models from together.ai"

**who said this?** the wish says "research and append the latest models" and "include all origins"

**what evidence supports this?** the wish explicitly calls out deepseek, qwen, etc. - implies coverage over completeness

**what if we didn't do this?** users would lack access to frontier models (deepseek v4 pro at 80.6% swe-bench) and ultra-cheap options (lfm2 at $0.03)

**verdict: holds** — but scoped to "notable" models, not exhaustive. we curate the best per tier, not every variant.

---

### 2. "fast & cheap models"

**who said this?** wish line 1: "specifically, focus on fast & cheap models"

**what evidence?** current config lacks ultra-cheap options. cheapest is qwen3-235b at $0.20 input. lfm2 at $0.03 is 7x cheaper.

**what if we didn't do this?** users who need high-volume, low-cost inference would stay on expensive models or leave the platform.

**could we achieve this simpler?** no — cheap models exist, we just need to add them.

**verdict: holds** — clear value gap filled by lfm2, gpt-oss, qwen3.5-9b, gemma4-31b.

---

### 3. "big & best models"

**who said this?** wish line 1: "as well as big & best models"

**what evidence?** current best is kimi k2.5 at 76.8% swe-bench. deepseek v4 pro hits 80.6%. kimi k2.6 hits 80.2%.

**what if we didn't do this?** users who need frontier quality would use proprietary models instead.

**verdict: holds** — gap to claude opus (80.8%) is now 0.2 points with deepseek v4 pro.

### 4. "include vision models"

**who said this?** not explicitly in wish. inferred from "all origins" + gemma 4 and qwen 3.5 have vision.

**what evidence?** multimodal code review is a real usecase. gemma 4 31b and qwen 3.5 397b support images.

**what if we didn't do this?** miss emerging usecase. low cost to include since they also do text.

**is scope misdirected?** possibly — vision may deserve a separate config pattern (image input handling).

**verdict: include but flag as open question** — vision capability extant but may need distinct contract support later.

## non-issues (why they hold)

follows established pattern from current config. consistent with brain config pattern brief. enables autocomplete by family (together/deepseek/*).

### swe-bench as primary benchmark

de facto standard for code capability. alternative benchmarks (livebench, terminal-bench) exist but swe-bench verified is most cited.

### hard-coded prices in BrainSpec

necessary evil — prices must be captured somewhere. version bumps signal price changes. changelog documents deltas.
